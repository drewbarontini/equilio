# Value Creation

**Create meaningful value.**

Value Creation is the product Model for connecting customer needs, business value, and product execution into a coherent whole.

## Formula

**Value = Intuition × Forging**

Intuition senses meaningful signals. Forging turns those signals into something new that can meet reality.

## Principles

### Name the real problem

Begin with observation, not assumption. Gather enough context to understand what the problem is, why it matters, and why it deserves attention now.

Problem fidelity creates contrast. The clearer the real problem becomes, the easier it is to compare opportunities and choose where to invest.

### Shape with intention

Give the solution enough form to align the team without pretending the unknowns have disappeared. Use clear constraints, rough sketches, words, and simple flows to define the boundaries of the work while preserving room to learn.

Shaping should clarify:

- The problem being solved.
- The time or energy worth investing.
- The essential value the solution must deliver.

### Ship early and often

Build small, complete slices that reach the user and create real feedback. A vertical slice is more truthful than isolated layers of apparent progress because it exposes the tensions between strategy, design, and engineering.

Each iteration should reduce uncertainty, deliver value, or teach the team what to do next.

## Practices

- **Product Forge** — Organize possibilities, bets, and increments of delivered work.
- **Value Engine** — Connect what the team creates to the value it delivers.
- **Value Iterations** — Release small, end-to-end increments that users can experience.

Practices are examples, not requirements. Adapt them to the product, team, and environment.

## Questions

- What signal deserves our attention?
- What is the real problem beneath the request?
- Why is it important to solve now?
- What is the smallest complete expression of value?
- What uncertainty can only be answered by doing the work?
