# Quality Refinement

**Refine through iteration.**

Quality Refinement is the product Model for continuously improving quality through shared standards, feedback, and refinement.

## Formula

**Quality = Integration × Refining**

Integration brings disciplines and context together. Refining removes friction and strengthens what remains.

## Principles

### Surface the signal

Products generate more information than a team can act on. Translate feedback, behavior, errors, inconsistencies, and intuition into clear signals that point toward a meaningful improvement.

The goal is not to respond to every input. It is to distinguish what matters from what is merely loud.

### Move with shared context

Quality degrades through disconnected decisions and lossy handoffs. Bring strategy, design, and engineering into the work together so the team can make decisions with a shared understanding of the whole product.

Document context, discuss it together, and preserve continuity as the work moves from idea to implementation.

### Increase the contrast

Make small changes in context and compare them quickly. Tight feedback loops make differences easier to feel, sharpen taste, and reveal which change actually improves the experience.

Quality is easier to judge when the before and after remain close enough to compare.

## Practices

- **Editorial Passes** — Review the product with a red pen; remove what does not serve a clear purpose.
- **Experience Unification** — Find seams and make the product feel like one coherent whole.
- **Quality Iterations** — Reserve a recurring cadence for removing, simplifying, and improving.

Practices are examples, not requirements. Adapt them to the product, team, and environment.

## Questions

- What is creating friction or obscuring value?
- What can we remove before adding something new?
- Where has the experience drifted or developed seams?
- Which disciplines need to review this together?
- What small change would create the clearest contrast?
