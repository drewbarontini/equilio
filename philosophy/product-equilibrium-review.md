# Product Equilibrium Review

Use this review to sense what the product needs next. Keep it grounded in current evidence rather than trying to assign equal weight to every Model.

## Signals

What are we noticing across customers, the product, the business, and the team?

Which signals are meaningful, recurring, or unusually strong?

## Current Balance

Where is the greatest need right now?

- **Value Creation** — Build or discover something new.
- **Quality Refinement** — Remove, simplify, unify, or improve what exists.
- **Strategic Momentum** — Restore clarity, rhythm, feedback, or forward movement.

What evidence supports that assessment?

## Real Problem

What is happening beneath the request or symptom?

Why does it matter now?

## Subtraction

What can we remove, simplify, stop, or defer before adding something new?

## Next Iteration

What is the smallest complete move that can create value or useful contrast?

What boundaries will keep it focused?

## Feedback

How will this move meet reality?

Who needs to experience or review it, and how quickly can that happen?

## Learning

What changed in the product?

What changed in our understanding?

What should we create, refine, or amplify next?
