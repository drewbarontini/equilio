# Strategic Momentum

**Build strategic momentum.**

Strategic Momentum is the product Model for creating sustained progress through alignment, prioritization, and purposeful execution.

## Formula

**Momentum = Iteration × Accelerating**

Iteration creates learning through repeated contact with reality. Accelerating compounds that learning into sustained forward movement.

## Principles

### Find your flow

Design a way of working around the rhythms of the team, product, and business. Align individual energy around shared purpose, then choose cadences, rituals, and tools that support the work.

Flow is contextual. It should create enough stability to coordinate without becoming a rigid process the team performs for its own sake.

### Move in fast loops

Make a change, gather feedback, and adjust while the context is still alive. Close collaboration and small, complete iterations reduce the delay between learning and action.

Speed matters because it increases the number and quality of feedback loops, not because output is valuable by itself.

### Multiply what works

Notice where the product or team is already creating unusual value. Understand the underlying Pattern, then extend or repeat it where the same conditions apply.

Momentum compounds when a team amplifies demonstrated strengths instead of only repairing weaknesses.

## Practices

- **Product Heartbeat** — Maintain a recurring pulse of alignment and visibility.
- **Build Dynamics** — Organize work through forging, refining, and accelerating.
- **Adaptive Habitat** — Shape an environment in which integrated teams can respond and learn.

Practices are examples, not requirements. Adapt them to the product, team, and environment.

## Questions

- What rhythm fits this team and moment?
- Where is work waiting on a handoff or decision?
- What is the next small, complete iteration?
- How quickly can reality give us useful feedback?
- What is already working that we should amplify?
