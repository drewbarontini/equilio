# Equilio Flow

**How we work.**

Equilio Flow describes how product work emerges, takes shape, creates value, and generates learning. It is a continuous movement, **not a mandatory five-stage process**.

## Five Movements

| Movement | Core concept | Principle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sense** | Work Registry | Work emerges from reality. |
| **Form** | Work Formation | Give work the shape it needs. |
| **Decide** | Product Current | Judgment directs attention and the next move. |
| **Deliver** | Integrated Delivery | Teams create value. |
| **Learn** | Product Memory | Reality responds; understanding changes direction. |

### 1. Sense — Work Registry

Capture signals from customers, product behavior, the business, and the team. The Work Registry makes actionable work visible.

**Five Types:** Idea · Task · Fix · Improvement · Bet.

### 2. Form — Work Formation

Recognize relationships and clusters. Introduce only enough structure to coordinate the work.

- **Issue:** Atomic, actionable work.
- **Batch:** Related work grouped for focused attention.
- **Project:** Bounded, coordinated effort toward an outcome.

Issues do not need to become Batches; Batches do not need to become Projects. A straightforward Fix or Improvement can move directly into delivery.

### 3. Decide — Product Current

Use [Product Current](../focus/README.md) to understand strategic Themes, active Streams and their Bets, Enablers, Experiments, and relevant Memory. It helps teams concentrate attention on what matters.

**Product Current is a strategic filter, not an approval gate.** Not all work belongs to a Stream or needs to appear in Product Current. Everyday fixes and improvements can continue through the Work Registry.

### 4. Deliver — Integrated Delivery

Bring the relevant people, intelligence, and practices together to produce functioning software.

- **Bamboo Teams** — Work together across disciplines around an outcome.
- **Build Dynamics** — Forge new value, refine existing quality, and accelerate the capacity to deliver.
- **Project Engine** — Guide committed Projects through integrated execution and learning.

[Human Calibration](https://drewbarontini.com/newsletter/77-human-calibration/), [Intelligence Orchestration](https://drewbarontini.com/newsletter/76-intelligence-orchestration/), and [Product Heartbeat](https://drewbarontini.com/newsletter/63-product-heartbeat/) support the work throughout; they are not additional steps.

### 5. Learn — Product Memory

Observe the effects of shipping and testing. Capture the evidence, decisions, releases, and updates that change what the team understands.

**Feedback → Signals → Decisions → Releases → Updates**

Product Memory lives **within Product Current** as connected, living views. Learning can change the current focus, reshape work in progress, or surface a new signal.

## Experiments

In the working Linear practice, an **Experiment is a Task designated as an Experiment**. It tests uncertainty and informs decisions; it is not a fourth Work Form or a required phase. Experiments can contribute to a Stream, a Bet, or exploration outside active Streams. [Product Current](../focus/README.md) shows the strategically relevant active Experiments in their own section.

## The Continuous Loop

**Sense → Form → Decide → Deliver → Learn → Sense**

The sequence tells a story; the work does not need to pass through each movement in order. Focus informs decisions throughout, and feedback can change any part of the work.

The measure is not throughput alone. It is **coherent value created in the working product**.
