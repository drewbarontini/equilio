# Equilio Focus

**What matters now.**

Equilio Focus takes form in **Product Current**: a living, shareable synthesis of what we're working on, why it matters, and what we're learning.

Product Current has three sections:

- **Themes:** Five strategic priorities that define direction.
- **Work:** Three active **Streams**, plus independent **Enablers** and **Experiments**.
- **Memory:** Five connected views—**Feedback, Signals, Decisions, Releases, Updates**.

Each Stream has a **Theme, Title, Mantra, Metric**, and **Bets** in three positions: **Live, Building, Next**. A Bet is a **selected strategic investment**, not a particular Work Type or Form: it may be a Project, Batch, or Experiment. The work keeps its existing structure when elevated to a Bet.

Enablers strengthen shared capabilities. Experiments are an **Exploration Work Type** that tests uncertainty. Either can contribute to one or more Streams, or stand independently. Experiments can be Bets when the learning itself merits strategic investment; the independent Experiments section still provides visibility outside individual Streams.

Product Current is **not the Work Registry**. It selectively expresses strategic focus; routine Fixes and Improvements can continue through [Equilio Flow](../flow/README.md) without becoming strategic initiatives. Product Memory is part of Product Current, not a separate artifact.

## Stream View

**Product Current is the zoomed-out strategic overview; the Stream View is the zoomed-in working context.** A Stream sustains a strategic tension and an outcome worth pursuing, rather than prescribing a fixed roadmap of Projects.

A Stream View can live in a parent issue, a standalone document, or another shared workspace. Use five lightweight sections:

1. **Overview** — Theme, title, description, mantra, and North Star metric.
2. **Projects** — Links to coordinated work, including Projects selected as Bets.
3. **Batches** — Related Fixes and Improvements receiving focused attention, including any Batches selected as Bets.
4. **Experiments** — Linked tests relevant to this Stream, including any Experiments selected as Bets.
5. **Ideas** — Possibilities and shaped Proposals being considered, not yet committed.

Keep individual Fixes, Improvements, and Tasks in the team's work tracker. Link or nest them under the Stream where useful; don't duplicate them in the overview. Enablers and Experiments may support multiple Streams or none, so their independent sections in Product Current remain useful.

Work evolves with understanding: feedback can reveal related Issues, a Batch can address friction, and evidence can justify a dedicated Project—or make one unnecessary. A Proposal becomes a Project when selected for that form of investment. A Batch or Experiment can instead become a Bet without becoming a Project. **Maintain strategic continuity without prescribing the scale of the work.**

## Product Current Template

[Copy the Product Current template](product-current-template.md) into the team's shared workspace. Keep it concise and link to live work rather than duplicating the source of truth.

As evidence changes, revise the focus. [Equilio Philosophy](../philosophy/README.md) guides the judgment; Flow supplies new learning.
