# Equilio Focus

**What matters now.**

Equilio Focus takes form in **Product Current**: a living, shareable synthesis of what we're working on, why it matters, and what we're learning.

Product Current has three sections:

- **Themes:** Five strategic priorities that define direction.
- **Work:** Three active **Streams**, plus independent **Enablers** and **Experiments**.
- **Memory:** Five connected views—**Feedback, Signals, Decisions, Releases, Updates**.

Each Stream has a **Theme, Title, Mantra, Metric**, and **Bets** in three positions: **Live, Building, Next**. Bets represent strategic commitments, commonly delivered as Projects.

Enablers strengthen shared capabilities. Experiments reduce uncertainty; in Linear, they are **Tasks designated as Experiments**. Either can contribute to one or more Streams, or stand independently. Neither needs to be placed within a Stream.

Product Current is **not the Work Registry**. It selectively expresses strategic focus; routine Fixes and Improvements can continue through [Equilio Flow](../flow/README.md) without becoming strategic initiatives. Product Memory is part of Product Current, not a separate artifact.

## Stream View in Linear

**Product Current is the zoomed-out view; the Stream issue in Linear is the zoomed-in working view.** A Stream holds a strategic tension and an outcome worth pursuing, not a fixed roadmap of Projects.

Keep the Stream issue light, with five sections:

1. **Overview** — Theme, title, description, mantra, and North Star metric.
2. **Projects** — Links to the current commitments (the Bets shown as **Live / Building / Next** in Product Current).
3. **Batches** — Related Fixes and Improvements receiving focused attention.
4. **Experiments** — Linked Tasks testing assumptions relevant to this Stream.
5. **Ideas** — Brief possibilities that aren't commitments yet.

Individual Fixes, Improvements, and Tasks remain as the Stream's child Issues in Linear; don't reproduce them as a second list. Enablers and Experiments can also support multiple Streams or none, so their independent sections in Product Current remain useful.

This allows work to develop with understanding: feedback can reveal related Issues, a Batch can address friction, and evidence can justify a Project/Bet—or remove the need for one. **Maintain strategic continuity without prescribing the scale of the work.**

## Product Current Template

[Copy the Product Current template](product-current-template.md) into Linear or another living workspace. Keep it concise and link to live work rather than duplicating the source of truth.

As evidence changes, revise the focus. [Equilio Philosophy](../philosophy/README.md) guides the judgment; Flow supplies new learning.
